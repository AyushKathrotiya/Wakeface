# WakeFace

An alarm for iPhone that runs in Safari. It gets louder and louder until the front camera sees your face, up close and with both eyes open. The whole screen turns white as the selfie flash.

**Open it:** https://ayushkathrotiya.github.io/wakeface/

1. Open the link in Safari, then tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
2. Open WakeFace from the Home Screen and allow the camera.
3. Pick a time and tap **Set alarm and go to sleep**. Leave the app open on screen with the phone plugged in, and turn your volume and brightness up.

Face detection uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0), bundled in `vendor/`.
